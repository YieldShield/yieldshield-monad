## Speaking draft

**David**

Hi, I'm David, and this is my co-founder, Santi. We're building YieldShield for people who hold MON or shMON and want a clearer way to manage downside without selling all their exposure. That's the user group we're starting with.

**Santi**

A holder has two exit choices: take back the remaining asset, or surrender it for an eligible backing payout. Other participants supply that backing and receive a share of realized gains. They take the first-loss risk. The payout has limits and conditions, so we need both sides to understand the tradeoff.

We've built the Monad testnet app with MON and shMON flows, wallet onboarding, and seven funded test pools. The tokens have no monetary value.

**David**

I previously led ecosystem work at Web3 Foundation and founded a startup. We want to turn a complicated risk decision into something people can understand and use.

YieldShield existed before this hackathon. We reused its protocol foundation and built the Monad application, asset integrations and deployment around it. Our public repository explains the new work and the AI assistance we used.

**Santi**

Our next step is to watch six holders and four potential backing providers use it. The important question is whether holders value these exit choices and providers accept the economics. We want evidence from both sides before considering real funds.

We're using Metropolis to test that, improve the product and find the right first users.
